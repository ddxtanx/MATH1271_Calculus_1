\section{Challenge Problem}

Suppose $f(x)$ is some differentiable function and set $g(x) = \ln(f(x))$.
What is the derivative of $g(x)$ in terms of $f(x)$ and $f'(x)$?
Use what you find to compute the derivative of the function $h(x) = x^x$.

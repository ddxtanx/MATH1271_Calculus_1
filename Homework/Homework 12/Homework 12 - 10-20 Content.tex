\section{Challenge Problem}

Assuming that $f(x)$ is a differentiable function, at which points is $f^{-1}(x)$ differentiable?

Assuming it's second differentiable, at which points is $f^{-1}(x)$ second differentiable?

What about third differentiability?

Use the above work to guess a condition that would guarantee the existence of \textit{all} derivatives of $f^{-1}(x)$, assuming all derivatives of $f(x)$ exist.

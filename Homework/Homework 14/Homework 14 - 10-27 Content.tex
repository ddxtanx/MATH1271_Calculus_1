\section{Challenge Problem}

Find an example of two functions $f(x), g(x)$ so that:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item $f(0) = g(0) = 0$
  \item $\lim_{x \rightarrow 0} \dfrac{f(x)}{g(x)}$ exists
  \item $\lim_{x \rightarrow 0} \dfrac{f'(x)}{g'(x)}$ does not exist.
\end{enumerate}
(I promise that such a pair of functions does exist and they're not weird/esoteric).

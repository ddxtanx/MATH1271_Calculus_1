\section{Challenge Problem}
Suppose that you are running experiment where a population of bacteria grows according to the following function
$$P(t) = e^t + 10$$
where $t$ is the time since population introduction and $P(t)$ is the organic mass of the population measured in grams.

Additionally, suppose that the bacteria produce/consume energy at a level depending on their total organic mass according to
$$E(p) = 10\sin^2(p)$$
where $p$ is the organic mass of the population in grams, and $E$ is energy in Joules.

If your instruments measure that the bacteria are producing exactly $5$ Joules of energy,
what is the earliest possible time since population introduction that the experiment could have been running?

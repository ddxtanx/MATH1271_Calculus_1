\section{Challenge Problem}

Suppose that you drop a ball and know that it's height in meters
as a function of time (in seconds since you dropped it) by:
\begin{equation}
  h(t) = \begin{cases} 40 - 10t^2, & 0 \leq t \leq 2 \\ -10t^2 + 60t - 80, & 2 \leq t \leq 4 \\ -10t^2 + 90t - 200, & 4 \leq t \leq 5 \\ 0, & \textit{otherwise} \end{cases}
\end{equation}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item What is $\lim_{t \rightarrow 1} h(t)$?
  \item What is $\lim_{t \rightarrow 2} h(t)$?
  \item What is the instantaneous velocity of the ball at $t = 1$?
  \item What is the instantaneous velocity of the ball at $t = 2$?
\end{enumerate}
